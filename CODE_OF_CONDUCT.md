# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Target Tracking welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying open to correction.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact.
- Protect private information shared during collaboration.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the repository maintainer through the `mkcheung568` GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue for sensitive reports. The maintainer will review reports and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.
